TRAVEL FAQS

Requests for travel must be submitted

7 days prior for domestic travel

30 days prior for international travel

Breakfast
WIFI inclusive plans
Requests for late check-out,
if needed

Food allowance

You are entitled to an allowance based on the approved grading structure

You can combine a personal trip with a business trip, to be approved by your respective HOD, before the Travel desk makes any bookings.   

Did you know?

Hassle free transaction

-Depending on your trip duration, claim an advance voucher approved by the HOD. Supporting bills must be provided post the trip.
 

-Avoid using personal credit cards. However, in case of last-minute arrangements, an employee can use his/ her personal credit card and seek reimbursement later while submitting original bills.
 

- All currency used and leftover Forex balance with an expense report must be submitted within 7 days post return. All loose currency must be handed over to the travel desk.
