CONFIDENTIALITY

During the term of your employment with the Company or after the term of service by notice, discharge or otherwise make known or divulge in any manner whatsoever any information, which in the service of the Company, you have acquired as secret information concerning the products, operations, strategies, inventions, discoveries, designs, technical writing, business methods, or internal systems and procedures, transactions, finances or affairs of the Company. 

 

The release of confidential information, whether intentional or unintentional, can damage the business of Unitile and those individuals associated with it. If a team member violates this policy, disciplinary action will be taken, up to and including immediate termination and/or legal action as applicable. 
 

If you are unsure if something is confidential or have any questions please reach out to your reporting manager/HR.
